欢迎使用《紫微天命录》（以下简称“本游戏”）。本隐私权政策由开发者 Robert（以下简称“我们”）制定，用于说明您使用本游戏及相关服务时，相关信息可能如何被处理，以及您可以如何行使适用法律赋予的权利。

二、我们可能处理的信息

我们仅在提供游戏功能、保障服务安全、展示广告、改善游戏体验或遵守法律要求所必要的范围内处理信息。实际处理的信息取决于您使用的功能、应用版本、SDK 集成及相关配置。

1. 游戏使用及本地进度

本游戏无需登录即可游玩。游戏运行过程中可能在您的设备本地保存游戏设置、游戏进度或其他实现游戏功能所需的数据。此类本地数据是否与设备标识符关联、是否会在卸载应用或清除应用数据后保留，取决于具体实现和设备系统行为。

如果本游戏实际提供联网功能、云端存档、排行榜、在线活动或其他服务器功能，相关功能可能处理实现该功能所必需的游戏数据、请求信息及技术信息。请以当前版本实际提供的功能为准；如果没有使用相关功能，则不会仅因本条描述而产生相应的数据处理。

2. 设备、网络及诊断信息

根据游戏运行及 SDK 配置，本游戏或其集成服务可能处理设备型号和制造商、操作系统及版本、应用版本、语言、时区、网络状态、IP 地址、崩溃日志、性能诊断信息，以及应用或设备相关标识符。这些信息可能用于兼容性适配、故障排查、服务安全和性能优化。实际收集范围应以应用及 SDK 的真实行为为准。

3. 广告相关信息（Google AdMob）

本游戏集成 Google AdMob 广告服务。根据广告功能、用户设置、所在地区及 SDK 配置，Google 及其合作伙伴可能处理广告标识符（例如 Android 广告 ID，如可用）、IP 地址、设备和应用信息、广告展示、点击及互动记录、诊断信息，以及用于广告投放、频次控制、效果衡量和防止欺诈的信息。

个性化广告及相关数据处理可能受所在地区、适用法律、用户同意状态和设备设置影响。您可以在 Android 系统设置中管理广告隐私或广告 ID（具体选项因 Android 版本和设备而异），也可以通过 Google 提供的隐私设置管理相关广告体验。

4. 游戏分析信息（Unity Analytics，仅在实际集成或启用时适用）

如果本游戏实际集成并启用了 Unity Analytics，Unity 可能根据 SDK 版本及配置处理游戏内事件、功能使用情况、会话统计、游戏行为统计、应用或设备标识符、IP 地址及性能诊断信息，用于分析游戏使用情况和改进产品。如果本游戏未集成或未启用 Unity Analytics，则不会通过 Unity Analytics 处理上述分析数据。我们将在核实实际集成状态后，确保本条描述与应用实际情况一致。

5. 您主动提供的信息

如果您通过电子邮件联系开发者，我们会处理您主动提供的邮箱地址、邮件内容及您选择提供的其他信息，以便回复和处理您的请求。

三、信息使用目的

在适用法律允许的范围内，我们或为本游戏提供服务的第三方可能将相关信息用于以下目的：

  1. 运行游戏、保存本地设置和游戏进度，以及提供当前版本实际具备的游戏功能；
  2. 展示广告、管理广告频次、衡量广告效果及防止广告欺诈；

我们仅在实现本政策所述目的、提供游戏服务或履行法律义务所必要的期限内保留由我们控制的信息。由 Google、Unity 等第三方独立处理或存储的信息，其处理方式和保存期限以相应服务商的政策为准。

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ziwei Destiny (the “Game”). This Privacy Policy is provided by Robert (“we,” “us,” or “our”), the developer of the Game. It explains how information may be processed when you use the Game and related services, and how you may exercise your rights under applicable privacy laws.

This Policy applies to the Android application Ziwei Destiny and services directly related to it. Please read this Policy before using the Game.

The Game does not require registration or account login. You can launch and play the Game directly. Unless a future version expressly adds such features and provides notice, creating a user account is not a prerequisite for playing.

2. Information We May Process

We process information only as necessary to provide game features, maintain service security, display advertisements, improve the Game, or comply with legal requirements. The actual information depends on the features you use, the app version, SDK integrations, and their configurations.

2.1 Game Use and Local Progress

The Game can be played without logging in. While the Game is running, it may store game settings, game progress, or other data needed for gameplay locally on your device. Whether such local data is associated with a device identifier, and whether it remains after uninstalling the app or clearing app data, depends on the implementation and device operating system.

If the Game actually provides online features, cloud saves, leaderboards, online events, or other server-based features, those features may process game data, request information, and technical information necessary to operate them. The actual processing depends on the features available in the version you use. If a feature is not provided, this description does not mean that the corresponding data is collected.

2.2 Device, Network, and Diagnostic Information

Depending on the Game's operation and SDK configuration, the Game or integrated services may process device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, app version, language, time zone, network status, IP address, crash logs, performance diagnostics, and app- or device-related identifiers. This information may be used for compatibility, troubleshooting, service security, and performance improvements. The actual scope must reflect the app's and SDKs' behavior.

2.3 Advertising Information (Google AdMob)

The Game integrates Google AdMob. Depending on advertising features, user settings, region, and SDK configuration, Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID, where available), IP address, device and app information, ad impressions, clicks and interactions, diagnostics, and information used for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Personalized advertising and related processing may depend on your region, applicable law, consent status, and device settings. You may manage advertising privacy or the Advertising ID in Android settings where available; options vary by Android version and device. You may also use Google's privacy settings to manage your advertising experience.

2.4 Game Analytics (Unity Analytics, Only If Integrated or Enabled)

If Unity Analytics is integrated and enabled in the Game, Unity may process in-game events, feature usage, session statistics, gameplay statistics, app or device identifiers, IP address, and performance diagnostics, depending on the SDK version and configuration. This information may be used to understand game usage and improve the product. If Unity Analytics is not integrated or enabled, the information described in this section is not processed through Unity Analytics. We will verify the actual integration status and ensure this description reflects the released app.

2.5 Information You Provide

If you contact the developer by email, we will process the email address, message contents, and any other information you choose to provide so that we can respond to and handle your request.

Game settings and progress may be stored locally on your device.
